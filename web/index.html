Alive Things

Pick up a cup. Be quiet for ten minutes. It comes alive.

  1. 1
    Find the cupIt is sitting on your table. Pinch to pick it up and turn it.
  2. 2
    Draw with one fingertipSlowly. Every stroke appears six times and closes into a pattern on its own. You don't need to know how to draw.
  3. 3
    Stay a whileHold still and the ink blooms. Nothing can go wrong here.
