Alive Things

Pick up a cup. Be quiet for ten minutes. It comes alive.

  1. Pinch the cup, or trace around anything on your desk. It comes to you, big.
  2. Draw with one fingertip, slowly. Every stroke appears six times.
  3. Set it down. It wakes, and lives in a garden you can always come back to.
