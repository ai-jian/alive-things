Alive Things

Pick up a cup. Be quiet for ten minutes. It comes alive.

  1. Bring something inA cup waits on your table. Or pinch in the air and trace around any object on your desk: it comes in at its real size and colour.
  2. Draw with one fingertipSlowly. Every stroke appears six times and closes into a pattern on its own. You don't need to know how to draw.
  3. Let it goHold it in one hand, pull with the other, and the room goes quiet. Open your hand: it wakes, and lives in a little garden you can always come back to.

Hands only, no controllers. Made for Meta Quest 3: open this page in the headset browser.

On a desktop or phone?

This page carries a Quest emulator. Press Enter, then drag on the cup with the mouse to paint. Keys: t trace a thing · f focus · w wake · g garden.
