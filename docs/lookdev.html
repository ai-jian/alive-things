What this proves

Soft lighting on a kept-texture object reads as cozy, not clinical. The jelly dent is a vertex shader, no physics. Paint is a decal canvas over the photo texture. All three port straight to Unity.

What it doesn't show

  • Bloom and depth of field (easy in Unity URP)
  • Passthrough behind the object
  • Hand models
