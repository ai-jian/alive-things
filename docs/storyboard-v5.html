50:32–0:48

Each unit lands with a soft tick. The line is a lighter tint of the cup's own colour, never a foreign colour. This shot is long and quiet on purpose.

Shot

70:58–1:06

Shot
You pinch the big cup and lower it toward the table. As it passes your chest it shrinks back to cup size, lands, and the fog lifts. A beat. It blinks. Little feet, little arms. A tag floats up: "quiet · curious". Those words came from how you drew: slow lines, long pauses.
Text
"It learned you while you drew."
Build
Lowering below chest height ends the studio: scale back, land on the table, wake, traits from stroke stats. Done.

What changed from v4

  • No more "hold with one hand, pull with the other". One pinch does everything: it grows in your hand, floats up big when you let go.
  • The size contrast is the key visual: a 9 cm cup on the desk, a 30 cm canvas in front of you, back to 9 cm on the table.
  • Objects react before you touch them (glow, turn, lean).
  • Tracing your own object is its own shot (8).
  • Colours are the gradient look test's palettes; strokes are a tint of the body, never a new colour.
