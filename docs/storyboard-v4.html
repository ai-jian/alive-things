Close. A fingertip drags slowly across the cup. The stroke appears six times at once, mirrored around a centre, and closes into a flower-like shape on its own. Each unit lands with a soft tick. The line bends itself to follow the curve of the cup. This shot is long and quiet on purpose. (Surface look: see art-direction, open.)

Zen brush with radial symmetry guide: pattern units along the stroke ×N, flow-line snapping, slow = more. Tick + haptic per unit.
60:48–0:58
No eraser. Water instead.
Shot
A flat palm wipes across one stroke. It doesn't vanish, it softens and spreads. Quick cuts: strokes, a swirl, dots, each one multiplied by the guide. You turn the cup and the pattern wraps all the way round. It is warmer now, breathing very slightly.
Text
"Nothing is a mistake here."
Build
Water gesture = soften. Six gesture-mapped patterns. Warmth and breathing scale with coverage.

Three pillars, one each act

  • Room: your real object becomes yours (shots 1–3)
  • Focus: quiet flow, anyone can draw (shots 4–7)
  • My Space: they live without you (shots 8–10)

What this board tells you to build

  • Passthrough, hands, glow-on-approach
  • Capture → inflated mesh (with ready-object fallback)
  • Hold-and-open gesture, Focus Mode
  • Zen brush: radial symmetry guide, 6 patterns, flow snapping, water
  • Surface look: A (bleed + palette) or B (relief). Open.
  • Traits from strokes, wake animation
  • Creature brain, whisper bubbles, affinity
  • My Space scene, light pools, plant guide

Not in any shot, so not yet

  • Saving, sharing, multiplayer
  • Hide-and-seek as a quest (one peeking newcomer is enough)
  • Music. Ambient hum + chirps only
  • Menus. Everything is a pinch, a pull, a palm

Shooting tips

  • Shot 5 is the heart. Let it be slow. Don't cut early.
  • Shot 10 is the thumbnail.
  • Shots 3 and 7 are the "wow" and the "aww".
  • Real desk, real mess, afternoon light.
