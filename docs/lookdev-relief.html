What this proves

Carving instead of colouring removes "ugly" from the user's hands. The clay is one colour. Patterns are relief. A three-band toon ramp plus a rim light makes the relief read from any angle, and turning the cup in your hand becomes the way you look at your own work.

How it works

  • Height map painted by the brush (signed: ridges and grooves)
  • Vertex displacement from height, normal from height gradient
  • Toon ramp: 3 bands, hard specular dot, cool rim
  • Optional glaze colour pools in the grooves
