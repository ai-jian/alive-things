What this proves

Radial symmetry is the Procreate trick that made zen doodling feel good: one stroke, six perfect copies, and the whole thing closes into a mandala on its own. On a 3D object the guide can sit on the front (mandala) or wrap around the axis (around the cup), and you turn the object to see it close.

How colour works

  • You paint intensity, not colour
  • Intensity bleeds like wet ink while the stroke is fresh
  • A palette gradient maps intensity to colour (deep where thin, hot where layered)
  • So layering slowly makes it bloom; nothing you do can clash

Try

  • Mandala, 6, Wet: draw one slow curve from the centre outward
  • Around the cup, 8: short strokes, then orbit
